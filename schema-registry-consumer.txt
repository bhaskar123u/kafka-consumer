Till now, this is what we have done and our producer and consumer is working fine. Below are the use-cases where this will fail:
1. Producer has increased version and now have an extra field say 'double totalAmount' and consumer is on older version where this field is missing. During de-serialisation, Jackson might ignore this, and it might get set to default value.
2. Producer might refer to v2 where a field has been removed but consumer is still referring to v1 and this might also cause data-loss.
3. Renaming a field in v2, can cause a silent loss of data.
4. Change of field type in v2, Jackson might throw exception.
5. No validation with old consumers of a new consumer wants a different structure and Producer starts producing according to the new required schema.

Solution : schema registry
Avro, Protobuf, Json Schema
It brings validation before producer send/publish and event. Provides a formal definition of structure of data.
